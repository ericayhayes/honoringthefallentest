#### About

Frank A. Young graduated from Villanova University in 1980 with a degree in Sociology. While at Villanova, he was part of the NROTC program, pursuing the Marine Option. After graduating from Villanova, Young earned the rank of First Lieutenant in the United States Marine Corps. On February 10, 1984, Young was reported missing around 4 PM while flying an NAS Meridian TA-AJ Skyhawk plane during an advanced strike training flight. His plane became disoriented in the clouds, entered a spiral, and crashed about 100 yards from a house near Jachin in Choctaw County, Alabama. Ejection was initiated but was not cleared before the plane made impact with the ground, and Lieutenant Young was killed in the crash.
